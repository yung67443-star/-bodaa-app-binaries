🛒 Customer & Passenger Support

How is my Snack Food Delivery fee calculated?
Delivery fees are computed dynamically based on the street route parameters generated from the vendor's storefront straight to your physical delivery destination via the OpenRouteService engine, matching our KSh 60 standard base minimum floor targets.
My ride estimation looks incorrect, what should I do?
If your GPS coordinates drop out during active tracking transactions, our system switches to a hardware device geometric fallback system including a 1.25x detour coefficient modifier to keep values accurate.

🏪 Vendor & Merchant Support

How do I manage my product catalog menu stock?
Open your merchant menu manager panel fragment dashboard layout wrapper. Toggling the active menu item MaterialSwitch automatically modifies your document's "inStock" status inside Firestore to shield empty orders.
