Frequently Asked Questions

Get instant answers to common workflow questions across our community lines.

✨ For Passengers

How do I order a snack?

Open your main Passenger App dashboard and select the 'Food Delivery' service tab. Browse the catalog listings from local restaurants or bakeries near you, add items to your cart, confirm your delivery address pin coordinates, and complete checkout via M-Pesa or your BodaWallet.

How does the School Trip service guarantee safety?

All drivers assigned to the school transit catalog undergo separate physical background checks and licensing verification. Once active, the parent app receives live background GPS notifications continuously, providing real-time ETA updates until arrival.

💼 For Driver Partners

How do I start earning?

Download the Driver App APK directly from this portal. Install the package, fill in your profile registration details, and upload your national ID, driver's license, and motorbike insurance documents. Once verified by our administrators, you can toggle your dashboard state to 'Online' to accept incoming requests.

🏪 For Food Vendors & Merchants

How are my cashout payments met?

All customer order funds are tracked continuously in your Merchant app profile ledger balances. Payout cashout settle requests can be initiated directly from your app panel, triggering an automated settlement deposit to your registered business M-Pesa line.
